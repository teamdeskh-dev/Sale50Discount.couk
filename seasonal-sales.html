Making sense of seasonal sales

What end-of-season and clearance events usually mean for prices and stock.

Why retailers clear stock

Holding stock costs money. When a season ends, remaining items are worth less to the retailer than the space they occupy, so they are reduced to move. This is the most reliable kind of reduction.

The trade-offs

  • Limited sizes and colours.
  • The biggest reductions are often on the less common sizes.
  • Clearance items may be final sale.
  • Delivery can slow during busy periods.

Clearance vs. continuous sales

A clearance line is reduced once and disappears. A line that is always on sale is part of normal pricing.

Is waiting worth it?

If you need the item now, buy now. If it is seasonal and you can wait, a season-end reduction often helps, provided your size stays in stock. There is no certainty, so treat waiting as a preference, not a guarantee.
