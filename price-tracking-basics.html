Price tracking basics

How to watch a single item over time to learn its real selling price.

Start with one item

Pick something you expect to buy. Watch only that item. One well-understood price history beats vague impressions of “sales”.

What to record

  • The price, including delivery.
  • The date.
  • The exact model and condition.
  • Whether the price needed an account or code.

Reading the history

If the item sits near the sale figure most of the time, the discount is permanent. If it genuinely sits high and dips occasionally, a dip is worth acting on.

Turning it into a decision

Set the price you will pay. When the item reaches it, buy; otherwise wait. That removes the urgency and the guesswork.
