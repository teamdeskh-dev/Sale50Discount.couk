How to verify a discount before you trust it

A short checklist for telling a real reduction from a relabelled price.

1. Find the normal price

The “was” figure is often the recommended price, not the price it sells at. If the item is sold near the discounted figure most of the time, the reduction is cosmetic.

2. Compare the final total

Add delivery, fees and any required subscription. A reduction that disappears once delivery is added is not a saving on your basket.

3. Check what the discount depends on

Some reductions need an account, membership, minimum spend or first order. Others exclude the variant you want. Read the terms so you compare the offer you can actually get.

4. Match the item precisely

Compare the same model, condition and extras. A cheaper, subtly different item is a different purchase.

5. Decide on the total

Once you know the normal price and the final total, the percentage stops mattering. Buy if the total is what you were willing to pay; skip it otherwise.
