Home & Garden Deals

Big-ticket home and garden purchases reward patience and clear measurement more than speed.

Clearance and end of season

Garden furniture, tools and seasonal goods are reduced when the season ends. Discounts are often real, but stock and return rights shrink.

Delivery and installation

The price on the tag is rarely the final cost. Delivery, assembly and old-item removal change the real total, so compare the delivered price.

Refurbished and returns

Ex-display and returned goods can be good value with a full warranty, but check the grading and whether the item can be returned.

Before you buy

  • Check the dimensions against the space and doorways.
  • Confirm delivery, assembly and recycling costs.
  • Read the returns and warranty terms.
Note: Prices, discounts and codes change constantly and can differ by region, account and basket. Everything here is general information, not an offer. Always confirm the final price and the terms at checkout before you buy.
