Clothing Deals

Clothing is where sale labels are loudest and often mean the least. Here is how reductions really behave.

End-of-season sales

Retailers clear stock at the end of a season. The reductions are usually real, but the popular sizes and colours go first, so the biggest percentage is often on what few people wanted.

Outlet and clearance lines

Some lines are made for outlet and are never sold at the reference price. Judge them by the price asked, not the price crossed out.

Multibuy on clothes

“Three for two” lowers the unit price only if all three fit and get worn. Otherwise you bought one item and two spares.

Before you buy

  • Check the fabric and care label, not just the price.
  • Confirm the returns window, as clearance is often final sale.
  • Compare the exact garment, not a similar one.
Note: Prices, discounts and codes change constantly and can differ by region, account and basket. Everything here is general information, not an offer. Always confirm the final price and the terms at checkout before you buy.
