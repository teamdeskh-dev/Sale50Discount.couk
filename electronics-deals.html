Electronics Deals

Electronics prices move in cycles. Knowing where you are in the cycle matters more than the discount percentage.

The price cycle

New models launch high, fall as they age, and drop sharply when a successor arrives. A big reduction on last year's model is normal, not a warning sign.

Bundles and extras

Bundles blend the product with accessories, warranty or credit. Compare the component prices individually before deciding the bundle saves anything.

Refurbished and open-box

Manufacturer-refurbished stock can be good value with a real warranty. “Seller refurbished” is not the same thing, so check the grade and who provides the warranty.

Watch the model number

  • Match the exact model string, not the marketing name.
  • Check the stored capacity and condition.
  • Confirm whether the price includes the accessories shown.
