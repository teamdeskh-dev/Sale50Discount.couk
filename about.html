About Sale50Discount

We are a small education project. Our aim is simple: explain the mechanics of shopping discounts clearly, so people can decide for themselves instead of being pushed by pressure tactics.

Why we exist

Percentage signs and countdown banners are designed to make a purchase feel urgent. A 50% label tells you almost nothing on its own. It does not tell you what the price was last month, whether the item is ever sold at the “full” price, or whether a cheaper equivalent exists elsewhere.

What we publish

  • Savings guides that walk through checking a discount, comparing products and timing a purchase.
  • Explanations of how percentage cuts, multibuy, coupon codes, cashback and price matching work.
  • Category notes for clothing, electronics and home & garden, where sales behave differently.
  • A journal of longer articles on sales cycles and price tracking.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell products or run a shop.
  • We do not publish or supply coupon codes.
  • We do not guarantee any discount, price or saving.
  • We do not present illustrations as photographs of real places or people.

How to use this site

Treat it as a checklist, not an authority. Prices and terms change, and they vary by region, account and basket. If something here does not match what you see at a retailer, trust the retailer.

Note: Prices, discounts and codes change constantly and can differ by region, account and basket. Everything here is general information, not an offer. Always confirm the final price and the terms at checkout before you buy.
