A simple routine you can reuse

  1. Note the price now, including delivery and fees.
  2. Check the history. If it usually sells near the “discounted” price, the label is cosmetic.
  3. Match like for like. Compare the same model, condition and warranty.
  4. Read the terms. Check returns, exclusions and whether a membership is needed.
  5. Decide on the final total, not the percentage.
Note: Prices, discounts and codes change constantly and can differ by region, account and basket. Everything here is general information, not an offer. Always confirm the final price and the terms at checkout before you buy.
