Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 May 2026

This policy explains how Sale50Discount handles personal data. We aim to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

  • Information you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and your message.
  • Technical information. Our host may record basic request data such as IP address, pages requested and browser type, for security.
  • Analytics. If enabled, aggregated usage data may be collected. It is not used to identify you.

How we use it

  • To reply to your message.
  • To keep the site secure and available.
  • To understand which content is useful.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us run the site, on our instructions.

Retention

We keep emails only as long as needed to handle your enquiry, then delete them. Server logs are kept briefly for security.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing. To make a request, use the contact page.

Cookies

See our Cookie Policy.
