Savings Tips

Small habits that reliably lower what you pay, without depending on any single code or offer.

Set a price alert

Let the price come to you, and remove the urgency that drives rushed spending.

Use the basket, then wait

Adding an item and waiting a day separates wanting it from being pushed to buy.

Read the signup offer

Welcome discounts are usually first-order only and carry exclusions.

Check what can be returned

A cheap item you cannot return is not a saving. Re-check the returns window.

Habits that reliably help

  1. Compare the final total, including delivery and fees.
  2. Note the normal price before a sale so the “was” figure means something.
  3. Match the exact item, not a similar-looking one.
  4. Read the exclusions before the headline percentage.
  5. Ask if you would buy it without any discount at all.
Note: Prices, discounts and codes change constantly and can differ by region, account and basket. Everything here is general information, not an offer. Always confirm the final price and the terms at checkout before you buy.
