Sale cycles, explained

Why sale events repeat through the year and what that means for timing a purchase.

The retail calendar

Most categories have predictable clearing points: end of season, end of a financial period, a new model, and a few large promotional events. Prices fall around those points and firm up in between.

Why prices rise after a sale

A sale clears stock and creates demand for its replacement. When it ends, the item returns to its normal price and can even rise if stock is low.

Using the cycle without pressure

  • Track one item long enough to see its normal and sale price.
  • Note the season-end points for that category.
  • Decide your price in advance.
  • Remember a lower price on something unneeded is still a spend.

Timing is context, not a guarantee

Cycles describe tendencies. Stock, popularity and supply can override them in any year, so treat timing as one input, not a rule.
