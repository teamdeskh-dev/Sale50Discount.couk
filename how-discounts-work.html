How Discounts Work

Discounts are rules and reference points. Once you see how each type is built, the label stops doing the thinking for you.

Percentage off

A percentage is relative. Thirty percent off a raised price can cost more than ten percent off a fair one. The only reliable comparison is the price now against what the item normally sells for.

Multibuy and bundles

Buy-two-get-one and bundle deals reduce the unit price only if you wanted the extra items. If you did not, the “saving” is extra spending.

Coupon codes

A code is a rule the checkout checks your basket against: a minimum spend, excluded categories, a validity window, a first-order condition. A valid code that does not apply to your basket is working as intended.

Cashback and rewards

Cashback returns a percentage later, often after a waiting period, and depends on the purchase tracking correctly. What you get can differ from the headline figure.

Price matching

Price match promises the same price as a named competitor for an identical, in-stock item. The catch is the definition of identical.

What to do instead

  • Compare the final total, including delivery and fees.
  • Check the real price history, not the “was” figure.
  • Ask whether you would buy it without the discount.
  • Read the exclusions before the headline number.
Note: Prices, discounts and codes change constantly and can differ by region, account and basket. Everything here is general information, not an offer. Always confirm the final price and the terms at checkout before you buy.
